This is a second sample post. It is here so the home page and the Writing page have more than one thing to show.

Short notes are the most common thing here. They are usually one idea, written down before it fades.

> The best note is the one you actually wrote.

A few things a short note can hold:

1. A problem and the fix
2. A link worth keeping
3. A question to come back to

That is the whole shape of it.
