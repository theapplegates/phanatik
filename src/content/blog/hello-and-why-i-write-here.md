This is a sample post. It exists so you can see how a real piece of writing looks on the page before you replace it with your own words.

Living Life is a place for notes on software, tools, and the work of making things. Some posts will be short. Some will be long. Most will sit somewhere in between.

## What to expect

- Short notes on things I learned
- Longer pieces when a topic deserves the room
- Links to tools and writing I found useful

## Why start

Writing something down is how I find out whether I actually understand it. This blog is the place where that happens in public.
